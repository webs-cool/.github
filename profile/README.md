# webs

**Your name. Your rules. Your internet.**

[webs](https://webs.cool) is a home for a site you actually own. Reserve a name, publish writing that search can still find next year, sell the work without a middleman, and keep the conversation in a guestbook you host.

A social post disappears in a day. A page on your name keeps working.

---

## What you get

| Piece | What it does |
|---|---|
| **[Name](https://webs.cool/claim)** | A `webs/yourname` address, or your own domain when you want it. |
| **Content engine** | Articles, with an editor that reads what people are looking for. |
| **Smart store** | Courses, guides, and access, sold from the same page. |
| **Guestbook** | A conversation you moderate. Your house, your tone. |
| **Branding** | A finished page from the first minute. |

The free page is a reserved name, writing by hand, and a live address at `webs/yourname`.

---

## The point

The web should belong to people.

- **Be found.** An article can bring people back for years.
- **Be independent.** Sell directly. The relationship stays yours.
- **Stay in charge.** You decide what the page says and who speaks on it.
